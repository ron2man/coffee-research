# Barista Pro

These claims were researched for the built-in grinder troubleshooter, the standalone-grinder guide, the clean/descale maintenance guide, the upgrade decision, the sour/bitter dial-in troubleshooter and the 'stops after a few seconds and beeps three times' troubleshooter. The current US instruction booklet (BES878 USCM Q26) was read page by page, including Assembly and First Use (pages 10–11, re-read 2026-11-27), Components and Accessories (pages 7–9), Grind Size, Dosing and Extracting Espresso (pages 13–16, read again on 2026-11-27), Shot Temperature, Reset Defaults, Hot Water and Texturing Milk (pages 16–17, re-read 2026-11-27), Care & Cleaning (pages 19–22) and Troubleshooting (pages 23–26, re-read 2026-11-27). The Sage BES878/SES878 multilingual quick guide (UG5, Nov 2019) was read for Care & Cleaning (pages 18–21). The Pro has no maintenance light and no pressure gauge: its LCD shows FLUSH and DESCALE alerts and a shot clock. The booklet has no beep-code list and no row for a cycle that cuts out after a few seconds with beeps. Breville's support article on that symptom could not be read (it renders client-side).
