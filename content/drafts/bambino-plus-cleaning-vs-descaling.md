# Breville Bambino Plus cleaning vs. descaling: which cycle do the lights mean?

If **1 CUP** and **2 CUP** take turns flashing on your US Breville Bambino Plus (BES500), run the **group-head cleaning cycle** with a cleaning disc and tablet. If **1 CUP and STEAM flash together, alternating with 2 CUP**, the machine is asking you to **descale**. Watch which buttons light up before pressing anything. The descale reminder lasts about 15 seconds and a button press can dismiss it. [Breville BES500 instruction book, p. 17](https://assets.breville.com/BES500/BES500_USCM_IB_Y25_LR.pdf#page=17)

## How do I know the cleaning cycle actually ran?

Put the cleaning disc and tablet in the portafilter basket and lock the portafilter in. Empty and reinstall the drip tray, fill the tank to MAX, and place a 2 L (68 fl oz) container beneath the portafilter and steam wand. With the cleaning alert showing, hold both cup buttons for five seconds. When both lights turn steady, press either cup button to start. The cup lights alternate during the flush. The machine returns to ready mode when the cycle finishes. Then remove and rinse the portafilter, disc, tray, and container. [Breville instruction book, p. 17](https://assets.breville.com/BES500/BES500_USCM_IB_Y25_LR.pdf#page=17)

## Can I run the cleaning cycle before the alert?

Breville’s US product page links to the Y25 booklet, which gives a shortcut for early cleaning. After setting up the disc, tablet, water, and container, turn the machine off so all lights are dark. Hold **2 CUP and STEAM** for five seconds to enter cleaning mode, then use either cup button to start when both cup lights are steady. The older US booklet linked below omits the shortcut. If your machine came with a different booklet, check its instructions before using the shortcut. The booklet difference alone can’t tell you which older machines accept it. [Current US product documentation](https://www.breville.com/en-us/product/bes500?sku=BES500BSS1BUS1) · [Y25 booklet, p. 17](https://assets.breville.com/BES500/BES500_USCM_IB_Y25_LR.pdf#page=17) · [Older US booklet, p. 15](https://www.breville.com/content/dam/breville/us/assets/miscellaneous/instruction-manual/espresso/BES500-instruction-manual.pdf#page=15)

## Is the pause during descaling normal?

Yes. Descaling treats mineral buildup inside the machine and includes a fresh-water rinse. For setup, follow the booklet’s descale steps, including removing the water filter if fitted before adding the descaler, using the DESCALE line and a 2 L waste container, and letting the machine cool completely. With the machine off, hold **1 CUP and STEAM** for five seconds. When those lights turn steady, press either button to start. [Breville instruction book, pp. 17–18](https://assets.breville.com/BES500/BES500_USCM_IB_Y25_LR.pdf#page=17)

When the solution phase pauses, refill the tank to MAX with fresh water and empty the waste container. Once **1 CUP** and **STEAM** are steady again, press either to run the rinse. The same two lights flash in alternation at the end, then the machine goes into standby. That return to standby is the cue that the full cycle is done. [Breville instruction book, p. 18, steps 7–11](https://assets.breville.com/BES500/BES500_USCM_IB_Y25_LR.pdf#page=18)

## What if the lights come back right away?

Check which lights are flashing and whether you saw the cycle reach its documented endpoint. If the machine still won’t complete the indicated cycle or immediately returns to the alert after completion, note the light pattern and where the cycle stopped. Contact Breville support with those details. The lights can identify the requested maintenance, but they don’t identify a failed part. [Breville instruction book, p. 3](https://assets.breville.com/BES500/BES500_USCM_IB_Y25_LR.pdf#page=3)
