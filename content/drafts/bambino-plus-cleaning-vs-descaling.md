# Breville Bambino Plus cleaning vs. descaling: which cycle do the lights mean?

If **1 CUP** and **2 CUP** take turns flashing on your US Breville Bambino Plus (BES500BSS1BUS1), run the **group-head cleaning cycle** with a cleaning disc and tablet. If **1 CUP and STEAM flash together, alternating with 2 CUP**, the machine is asking you to **descale**. Watch which buttons light up before pressing anything. The Y25 booklet linked from Breville’s US SKU page says the descale reminder lasts about 15 seconds. Pressing any of the flashing buttons dismisses the reminder. [US SKU page](https://www.breville.com/en-us/product/bes500?sku=BES500BSS1BUS1) · [Y25 booklet, p. 17](https://assets.breville.com/BES500/BES500_USCM_IB_Y25_LR.pdf#page=17)

| Cycle | Alert | Supplies | How to start | Done when |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Group-head cleaning | 1 CUP and 2 CUP take turns flashing | Cleaning disc, tablet, water to MAX, 2 L container | With the alert showing, hold both cup buttons for 5 seconds. When both are steady, press either. | The machine returns to ready mode. |
| Descaling | 1 CUP and STEAM flash together, then 2 CUP flashes | Descaler, water to DESCALE, fresh rinse water, 2 L container | With the machine off and cool, hold 1 CUP and STEAM for 5 seconds. When both are steady, press either. | After the rinse, 1 CUP and STEAM alternate, then the machine enters standby. |

## How do I know the cleaning cycle actually ran?

Put the cleaning disc and tablet in the portafilter basket and lock the portafilter in. Empty and reinstall the drip tray, fill the tank to MAX, and place a 2 L (68 fl oz) container beneath the portafilter and steam wand. With the cleaning alert showing, hold both cup buttons for five seconds. When both lights turn steady, press either cup button to start. The cup lights alternate during the flush. The machine returns to ready mode when the cycle finishes. Then remove and rinse the portafilter, disc, tray, and container. [Breville instruction book, p. 17](https://assets.breville.com/BES500/BES500_USCM_IB_Y25_LR.pdf#page=17)

## Can I run the cleaning cycle before the alert?

After setting up the disc, tablet, water, and container, turn the machine off so all lights are dark. Hold **2 CUP and STEAM** for five seconds, then press either cup button when both cup lights are steady. Breville’s Y25 and Q21 booklets both document this shortcut. The older edition hosted by Williams Sonoma omits it from its cleaning section. If your machine came with a different booklet, check its instructions before using the shortcut. Compatibility across older production runs remains unverified. [Y25 booklet, p. 17](https://assets.breville.com/BES500/BES500_USCM_IB_Y25_LR.pdf#page=17) · [Q21 booklet, p. 16](https://assets.breville.com/Instruction-Booklets/USCM/BES500_USCM_IB_Q21_FA_WEB.pdf#page=16) · [Williams Sonoma copy, p. 15](https://www.williams-sonoma.com/netstorage/pdf/142739-Breville-Bambino-Plus-Manual.pdf#page=15)

## Is the pause during descaling normal?

Yes. Descaling removes mineral buildup and pauses before the fresh-water rinse. Empty and reinstall the drip tray. Remove the water filter if fitted, fill the tank to DESCALE, and add the descaler. Place a 2 L (68 fl oz) container beneath both the portafilter and the steam wand. Turn the machine off and let it cool completely. Hold **1 CUP and STEAM** for five seconds, then press either button when both lights are steady. [Y25 booklet, pp. 17–18](https://assets.breville.com/BES500/BES500_USCM_IB_Y25_LR.pdf#page=17)

Keep the tank installed while the machine is pumping, and avoid draining it yourself. Wait for the machine to pause on its own after using the solution. At that pause, refill the tank to MAX with fresh water, reinstall it, and empty and return the waste container beneath both outlets. When **1 CUP** and **STEAM** are steady, press either to run the rinse. Descaling is complete when those lights alternate and the machine goes into standby. Then empty the container and rinse and dry the tank and drip tray before reinstalling them. [Y25 tank warning, p. 17](https://assets.breville.com/BES500/BES500_USCM_IB_Y25_LR.pdf#page=17) · [Y25 descale steps 7–11, p. 18](https://assets.breville.com/BES500/BES500_USCM_IB_Y25_LR.pdf#page=18)

## What if the lights come back right away?

Check which lights are flashing and whether you saw the cycle reach its documented endpoint. If the machine still won’t complete the indicated cycle or immediately returns to the alert after completion, stop using the machine and contact Breville Consumer Support. Tell them which lights you saw and where the cycle stopped. If the machine malfunctions at any point, stop using it immediately. [Breville instruction book, p. 3](https://assets.breville.com/BES500/BES500_USCM_IB_Y25_LR.pdf#page=3)
