# Linea Micra cup clearance with the stock portafilter

If your cup and scale are a tight fit under the La Marzocco Linea Micra, try the included portafilter in bottomless mode first. Removing the snap-on spout frees up space under the basket while keeping the same portafilter. If your cup still crowds the basket, pull the shot into a shorter glass on the scale and pour it into your serving cup. ## Which portafilter setup should you try?

La Marzocco’s US Micra comes with the convertible portafilter and two spout attachments. You can use one outlet, two outlets, or the exposed basket. | What you want to do | Setup to try |
|---|---|
| Weigh a shot in one cup and gain room above it | Remove the spout and center the cup beneath the basket. |
| Keep using a spout for one drink | Fit the single spout and check that the outlet clears the rim with the scale in place. |
| Split a shot between two cups | Fit the double spout and check both cups together. Each outlet needs to sit over its own cup, and both cups need room to slide out. |
| Use a tall mug that still won’t fit | Weigh the shot in a short glass or pouring cup, then transfer it to the mug. |

## How do you check whether your cup and scale fit?

Test with the machine cool and idle. Lock in the empty portafilter in the configuration you plan to use. Put your scale and any mat you normally brew on in position, then slide the empty cup underneath. Check that the cup goes in and comes out level, that its rim stays clear of the portafilter, and that the scale sits flat with its display visible. A cup that only goes in at an angle may be awkward to remove once it contains espresso.

If it’s close, measure from the top of the scale or mat to the lowest part of the portafilter over the cup’s path. Compare that distance with your cup’s height and leave enough room to move it comfortably. If you’re checking a machine before purchase, ask for that measurement with the exact spout setup you plan to use. The usable space depends on what you put above and below the cup.

## How do you put the spout back on?

La Marzocco’s workflow guide shows where the spout sits relative to the handle and describes twisting it down to lock. Use the demo to check the orientation before fitting it. After brewing, rinse away grounds and clean the basket and portafilter regularly to remove coffee oils.
