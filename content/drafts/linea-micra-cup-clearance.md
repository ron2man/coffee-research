# Linea Micra cup clearance with the stock portafilter

With the stock drip tray and convertible portafilter on the US La Marzocco Linea Micra, try bottomless mode first if your cup and scale are a tight fit. Removing the spout attachment frees up space under the basket while keeping the same portafilter. If your cup still crowds the basket, pull the shot into a shorter glass on the scale and pour it into your serving cup. [US Micra listing](https://home.lamarzoccousa.com/product/linea-micra/)

Verified clearance figures are unavailable for the three stock setups covered here. If you’re shopping, ask the seller to check the setup you plan to use against your cup and scale dimensions.

## Which portafilter setup should you try?

La Marzocco’s US Micra comes with the convertible portafilter and two spout attachments. You can use one outlet, two outlets, or the exposed basket. | Split a shot between two cups | Fit the double spout. Check that both cups sit flat at the same time, each opening lines up with its outlet, and both cups can slide out level. |
| Use a tall mug that still won’t fit | Weigh the shot in a short glass or pouring cup, then transfer it to the mug. |

## How do you check whether your cup and scale fit?

Test with the machine cool and idle. Lock in the empty portafilter in the configuration you plan to use. Set the scale and any mat where you’ll brew, with the scale flat and its display visible. Slide the empty cup into its brewing position on the scale and back out again, keeping it level. Check that the rim and handle clear obstructions along the whole path, with the handle turned the way you normally hold it.

For the double spout, place both cups in their brewing positions together. If you’re weighing both on one scale, check that the weighing platform supports both bases flat at the same time and that each outlet is over its own cup opening. Check that the rims and handles clear each other and the machine as you slide the cups in and out.

### What should you ask a seller to measure?

Give the seller your scale’s dimensions and usable platform size, any extra mat thickness, and your cup’s height, base width, rim width, and total width including the handle. Ask them to lock in the stock portafilter in your chosen mode and measure from the bare stock drip tray grate to the lowest obstruction the cup passes under on the way in or out, including at the brewing position. If they can try your gear or similarly sized pieces, ask them to repeat the level fit check with the cup in its actual position on the scale. For the double spout, ask them to check both paths, both bases sitting flat, and both openings aligned under the outlets at the same time.

Add the scale height, any mat thickness that raises the cup or scale, and the cup height. Subtract that stack height from the seller’s measurement. In an **illustrative example**, an 18 mm scale, a 2 mm mat, and a 55 mm cup make a 75 mm stack. A hypothetical 80 mm clearance leaves 5 mm of vertical room. Use the level fit check to confirm that the rim and handle clear and the cup can slide out when filled.

## How do you put the spout back on?

With the portafilter out of the machine, start with the spout angled toward the handle and offset to its right. Twist the spout downward until it locks. [La Marzocco’s written workflow guide](https://home.lamarzoccousa.com/?p=292461)

After brewing, rinse away grounds and clean the basket and portafilter regularly to remove coffee oils. [La Marzocco’s maintenance guide](https://home.lamarzoccousa.com/?p=397148)
