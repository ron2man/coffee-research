# Linea Micra cup clearance with the stock portafilter

With the stock drip tray and convertible portafilter on the US La Marzocco Linea Micra, try bottomless mode first if your cup and scale are a tight fit. Removing the spout attachment frees up space under the basket while keeping the same portafilter. If your cup still crowds the basket, pull the shot into a shorter glass on the scale and pour it into your serving cup. [US Micra listing](https://home.lamarzoccousa.com/product/linea-micra/)

## Which portafilter setup should you try?

La Marzocco’s US Micra comes with the convertible portafilter and two spout attachments. You can use one outlet, two outlets, or the exposed basket. | What you want to do | Setup to try |
|---|---|
| Weigh a shot in one cup and gain room above it | Remove the spout and center the cup beneath the basket. |
| Keep using a spout for one drink | Fit the single spout and check that the outlet clears the rim with the scale in place. |
| Split a shot between two cups | Fit the double spout and check both cups together. Each outlet needs to sit over its own cup, and both cups need room to slide out. |
| Use a tall mug that still won’t fit | Weigh the shot in a short glass or pouring cup, then transfer it to the mug. |

## How do you check whether your cup and scale fit?

Test with the machine cool and idle. Lock in the empty portafilter in the configuration you plan to use. Put your scale and any mat you normally brew on in position, then slide the empty cup underneath. Check that the cup goes in and comes out level, that its rim stays clear of the portafilter, and that the scale sits flat with its display visible. A cup that only goes in at an angle may be awkward to remove once it contains espresso.

### What should you ask a seller to measure?

Ask the seller to lock in the stock portafilter in the configuration you’ll use (single spout, double spout, or bottomless). Ask for the smallest vertical distance from the top of the bare stock drip tray grate to any obstruction along the cup’s path, from the front of the tray to the brewing position. For the double spout, ask them to check both cup paths.

Add the heights of your scale, any separate mat that raises the cup or scale, and the cup itself. Subtract that total from the seller’s measurement. Leave room to slide the cup out level once it’s filled. If the numbers are close, ask the seller to try your setup or a similarly sized cup and scale.

## How do you put the spout back on?

With the portafilter out of the machine, start with the spout angled toward the handle and offset to its right. Twist the spout downward until it locks. [La Marzocco’s written workflow guide](https://home.lamarzoccousa.com/?p=292461)
