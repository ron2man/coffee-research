# Fellow Opus espresso troubleshooting brief

- Status — Draft
- Research and search snapshot — 2026-11-21
- Audience — English-language US owners of an original Fellow Opus or Opus 2 whose espresso runs fast at the fine end of the dial.
- Reader decision — Check whether the shot setup or the grinder range needs attention, identify the generation and use its documented adjustment procedure.
