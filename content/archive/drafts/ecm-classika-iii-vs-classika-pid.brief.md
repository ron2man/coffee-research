## Reader takeaway

A UK buyer can identify which Classika a listing offers and use a manual-matched seller demonstration to decide whether the exact used PID fits their cold-start window.

The two ECM catalogue item numbers and the III’s control layout give the buyer concrete details to verify. The PID manual for CM100003-00 provides the display cues for the documented Fast Heat-Up sequence. The article asks for a timed run from cold switch-on through the first espresso, including the flush if required. This is a buyer’s check. No seller demonstration or controlled first-shot timing was observed during research.
