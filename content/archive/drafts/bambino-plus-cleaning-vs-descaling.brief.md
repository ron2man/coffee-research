Selected the Bambino Plus guide because the SKU-linked Y25 booklet supports a more specific contribution than the Encore ESP evidence. Breville’s product listing links to the manual, and the competing guides already cover much of the workflow. This guide brings the two cycles and the US booklet differences together.
