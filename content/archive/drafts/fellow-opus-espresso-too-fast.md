# Fellow Opus espresso still runs fast at the finest setting? Check which Opus you have

If your shot still runs fast at the finest setting, check the recipe and tamp first. Then check the grinder. On the **original Opus (Opus 1)**, the blue inner ring can extend the range finer. On the **Opus 2**, the side dial handles everyday adjustments, and Fellow has a separate screwdriver calibration procedure for cases where the dial reaches its fine limit. [Fellow’s Opus 1 ring guide](https://help.fellowproducts.com/hc/en-us/articles/12697812844315-What-does-the-Opus-1-inner-adjustment-ring-do-and-how-do-I-use-it) · [Fellow’s Opus 2 calibration guide](https://help.fellowproducts.com/hc/en-us/articles/56361337841691-What-to-Do-if-Your-Opus-2-Can-t-Grind-Fine-Enough-for-Espresso)

## What should I check before changing the grinder’s range?

Repeat a shot with the same coffee, basket, dose and target yield. Check that you tamped the grounds, that the machine dispenses the expected amount of water and that your shot program allows enough time to reach the target. A fast shot by itself leaves the cause open. If the shot still runs fast at the finest setting after those checks, use the instructions for your model. [Fellow’s checks before calibration](https://help.fellowproducts.com/hc/en-us/articles/56361337841691-What-to-Do-if-Your-Opus-2-Can-t-Grind-Fine-Enough-for-Espresso)

## How do I get a finer setting on the original Opus?

Record both ring positions so you can return to your starting point. Empty the bean bin, turn the outer ring to **6**, and twist the bin counterclockwise to remove it. Press both tabs on the blue ring evenly. With the tabs held down, turn the outer ring so the pointer on the inner scale moves toward **minus** for finer grinding. Release the tabs, check that the rings move together again, and fully reseat the bin. Fellow’s guide shows the movement on video. [Fellow’s Opus 1 adjustment instructions](https://help.fellowproducts.com/hc/en-us/articles/12697812844315-What-does-the-Opus-1-inner-adjustment-ring-do-and-how-do-I-use-it)

Return the outer ring to your espresso setting and pull another shot with the same recipe. The smaller number on the inner scale shifts the range of the entire outer ring. Fellow says six notches from the center toward minus move outer setting 1 one full numbered setting finer. [Fellow’s explanation of the Opus 1 range](https://help.fellowproducts.com/hc/en-us/articles/12697812844315-What-does-the-Opus-1-inner-adjustment-ring-do-and-how-do-I-use-it)

## What if my Opus 2 is already at 0?

If the shot still runs too fast after the setup checks, follow Fellow’s Opus 2 finer-calibration guide. It starts by unplugging the grinder and setting the dial to **0**. Have a small flathead and Phillips screwdriver ready and use the guide’s video or illustrated steps to access and adjust the calibration screws and reassemble the grinder. Then try the same coffee and recipe again so you can judge what changed. The setting that works will depend on your setup. [Fellow’s Opus 2 calibration instructions](https://help.fellowproducts.com/hc/en-us/articles/56361337841691-What-to-Do-if-Your-Opus-2-Can-t-Grind-Fine-Enough-for-Espresso)
