# Linea Micra cup clearance with the stock portafilter

With the stock drip tray and convertible portafilter on the US La Marzocco Linea Micra, try bottomless mode first if one cup and scale are a tight fit. Removing the spout attachment frees up space beneath the basket. If your cup still crowds the basket, weigh the shot in a shorter glass and pour it into your serving cup. [US Micra listing](https://home.lamarzoccousa.com/product/linea-micra/) · [noto studio’s clearance guide](https://noto-studio.de/blogs/news/linea-micra-cup-clearance)

If you split shots with the stock double spout, check both cups together. Each outlet should line up over its own cup, both bases should sit flat on the weighing platform if you use one scale, and both cups should slide out level.

The sources checked for this guide did not yield verified clearance measurements covering all three stock configurations. If you’re shopping, ask the seller to measure your chosen setup and, if possible, check the fit with your gear.

## Which portafilter setup should you try?

La Marzocco’s US Micra comes with the convertible portafilter and two spout attachments. You can use one outlet, two outlets, or the exposed basket. [US Micra listing](https://home.lamarzoccousa.com/product/linea-micra/) · [La Marzocco’s espresso workflow guide](https://home.lamarzoccousa.com/?p=292461)

| What you want to do | Setup to try |
|---|---|
| Weigh a shot in one cup and gain room above it | Remove the spout and center the cup beneath the basket. |
| Keep using a spout for one drink | Fit the single spout and check that the outlet clears the rim with the scale in place. |
| Split a shot between two cups | Fit the double spout. Check that both cups sit flat at the same time, each opening lines up with its outlet, and both cups can slide out level. |
| Use a tall mug that still won’t fit | Weigh the shot in a short glass or pouring cup, then transfer it to the mug. |

## How do you check whether your cup and scale fit?

Test with the machine cool and idle. Install the brew basket you intend to use, then lock the empty portafilter into the group head in your chosen mode. Set the scale and any mat where you’ll brew, with the scale flat and its display visible. Slide the empty cup into its brewing position on the scale and back out again, keeping it level. Check that the rim and handle clear obstructions along the whole path, with the handle turned the way you normally hold it.

For the double spout, place both cups in their brewing positions together. If you’re weighing both on one scale, check that the weighing platform supports both bases flat at the same time and that each outlet is over its own cup opening. Check that the rims and handles clear each other and the machine as you slide the cups in and out.

### What should you ask a seller to measure?

Give the seller your scale’s dimensions and usable platform size, any extra mat thickness, and your cup’s height, base width, rim width, and total width including the handle. Ask them to install your intended brew basket and lock the empty stock portafilter into the group head in your chosen mode. Have them measure from the bare stock drip tray grate to the lowest obstruction along the cup’s full insertion and removal path, including at the brewing position. For the double spout, ask for the measurement along each cup’s path.

Add the scale height, any mat thickness that raises the cup or scale, and the cup height. Subtract that stack height from the seller’s measurement. In an **illustrative example**, an 18 mm scale, a 2 mm mat, and a 55 mm cup make a 75 mm stack. A hypothetical 80 mm clearance leaves 5 mm of vertical room.

Finish with the physical fit check. Ask the seller to try your gear or similarly sized pieces, or do the check yourself on a machine you can try. Keep the intended brew basket installed and slide each cup in and out level along its full path. For the double spout, check both cups together for flat support, alignment under both outlets, and room for the rims and handles to pass. Confirm with your actual cups and scale before relying on the fit.

## How do you put the spout back on?

With the portafilter out of the machine, start with the spout angled toward the handle and offset to its right. Twist the spout downward until it locks. [La Marzocco’s written workflow guide](https://home.lamarzoccousa.com/?p=292461)

After brewing, rinse away grounds and clean the basket and portafilter regularly to remove coffee oils. [La Marzocco’s maintenance guide](https://home.lamarzoccousa.com/?p=397148)
