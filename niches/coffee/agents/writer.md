# Writer guidance: YourCoffeeSetup

## Manufacturer documents

- The same machines are sold as Breville in some markets and Sage in others. Never write "the Sage guide", "the Breville manual" or "the US booklet" in the body. Write "the manufacturer's quick guide", "the manual", or describe the edition by what the reader can see: "the manual for machines with the CLEAN ME light", "editions whose panel shows…". Name the specific regional documents only in the "How this guide was put together" section.
- When editions really differ, scope the difference to the panel, light, part or model number (`{{model:<product-id>}}`), never to a country.

## Product names

- Use tokens for every product, including ones mentioned in passing or as a comparison, such as the Barista Express Impress or the Smart Grinder Pro: `{{product:<product-id>}}`. If a product you want to mention has no card yet, describe it generically ("the manufacturer's standalone grinder") or leave it out, and note the missing card in `reason`.

## Buying caveats

- Don't give advice on plugs (UK/EU), voltage, warranty or importing for a given market ("buy from a UK retailer"). The offers box handles retailers per market.
