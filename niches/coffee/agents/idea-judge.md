# Idea judge: coffee niche notes

- Breville/Sage instruction booklets on assets.breville.com are compressed PDFs this environment can't read, and ManualsLib and similar mirrors return 403. Don't assume a manual covers Breville/Sage internals unless you have read a readable page. That includes the upper/inner-burr adjustment, which owners call undocumented. Breville support HTML pages (for example the clean/descale light page) are readable and can confirm coverage.
- OzBargain is an Australian deals forum. Its demand usually shows up in comments under deal posts, not as direct questions, and it is AU-only. All OzBargain threads count as one source. Pair them with a thread from another venue (r/espresso, Home-Barista, coffeeforums.co.uk) before scoring demandEvidence 4, and check that the market matches the card's.
- Sage is the UK/EU brand name for Breville. A Sage thread is a UK/EU signal, even for a card that targets the US.
