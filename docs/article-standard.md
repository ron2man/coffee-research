# YourCoffeeSetup article standard

Proposed house style for the public site. Use the Bambino grinder column as the buying-guide pilot.

## Reading experience

Use a single reading column. Put the headline and a quiet publication credit above the story. A buying column opens with a recognizable moment at the counter. Introduce the recommendation once the reader understands the trade-off. An owner troubleshooting a flashing light gets the meaning and immediate action first. Use section headings where the question changes. Reserve tables for things people actually look up.

| Format | Starting length | Finish when the reader can… |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Focused troubleshooting | 250–600 words | Recognize the symptom, take the right action and verify the result. |
| Buying column | 500–900 words | Choose a default, explain when another option fits, or confidently keep their equipment. |
| In-depth comparison | 900–1,500 words | Weigh meaningful differences using comparable evidence for their market. |

These are editing ranges. A complete 400-word buying column can ship. Extra words should earn their place through evidence or explanation. Google describes its people-first guidance as independent of a preferred word count. [Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content)

## Authors and dates

Use YourCoffeeSetup as the publication credit for an automated article. Link the credit to an editorial-method page explaining how the agent researches, writes and checks claims. Give a named contributor a linked byline or review credit only for work they actually performed. Include their relevant experience in their bio. Attribute firsthand observations to the person who made them. Show the original publication date and add an updated date when the advice materially changes.

## Images

For each proposed figure, write down what the reader should notice and where it belongs in the story. Prefer an original photograph of the exact equipment, a licensed photograph, or a manufacturer asset with documented permission for the intended commercial use. Use generated illustrations for explanatory concepts and label them as illustrations. Use verified photographs for model identification and physical controls.

Record the creator, source page, permission or license URL, date checked, and any crop or adaptation with the asset. Write alt text for the detail that matters, a caption when it helps interpret the image, and visible attribution as required. Use responsive image sizes and reserve the image dimensions on the page. If rights or model identity are unclear, omit the figure or use it only for a verified general subject with appropriate rights.

For the Bambino article, request one close-up of the undersides of the actual single-wall and dual-wall baskets, side by side. Place it where the reader is asked to lift out the basket. ## Sources, product cards and offers

Link to the manual or test beside the factual claim it supports. Link a model name to a public product card when the card adds something worth leaving the story for, such as identifying a revision or checking basket compatibility. The underlying research card remains the agent's evidence record.

After the case for a purchase is made, offer one compact product card with the exact model and variant, one sentence about who it suits, and a specific action such as “Check US availability at [retailer].” Use a market-matched merchant destination. Show prices only with a verified currency, market and check date. If no verified offer exists, the guide can still carry its recommendation. An owner who can solve the problem with their existing equipment needs no shopping prompt.

For a commission-earning link, explain the relationship in plain language near the first offer and set the link's rel attribute to sponsored. Keep editorial citations separate from tracked merchant links. Count outbound clicks by article, product and placement. Attribute a purchase only when a merchant reports it. [Google guidance on affiliate links](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2021/07/link-tagging-and-link-spam-update)

## Future website-agent handoff

The writer supplies the story, the applicable author or publication credit, suggested figures and the product IDs each recommendation relies on. The website agent resolves credits, licensed images and verified regional offers, then checks the rendered page on a phone and desktop. The judge sees the final captions, cards and disclosures alongside the prose. Track the first pilot by qualified outbound clicks and reader feedback before making any placement a site-wide default.
